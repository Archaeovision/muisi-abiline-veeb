Archaeovision (registry code 12705772, address Kaarli pst 5, Tallinn,
Estonia) publishes digital photographs and 3D models via the IIIF
(International Image Interoperability Framework) standard, enabling
them to be viewed and studied through various IIIF-compatible viewers
(e.g. Mirador, Universal Viewer). This page describes the terms under
which these images and models may be used.

## 1. Copyright

All images, photographs, and 3D models published through this service
are protected by copyright. Copyright belongs to Archaeovision or, where
a specific creator is named alongside the image, to that creator.

## 2. Permitted use

Images and models may be viewed, studied, saved, and shared for
non-commercial, educational, research, or cultural heritage purposes,
provided that:

- Archaeovision is credited as the source;
- where a specific creator is named alongside the image, that creator is
  credited as well;
- the image is not altered in a way that distorts its content or
  context.

## 3. Commercial use

For commercial use of images or models (including in print, media, or
commercial products), please contact Archaeovision in advance to obtain
separate permission.

## 4. Attribution

Suggested citation format: "Source: Archaeovision" or, where a creator
is named, "Creator: [name] / Archaeovision".

## 5. Contact

For questions, licensing requests, or anything else, please contact:
[support@archaeovision.eu](mailto:support@archaeovision.eu).

## 6. Changes

We may update this page as needed and will indicate the new effective
date at the top of the page.
